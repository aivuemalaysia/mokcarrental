## What YOU Need to Do Next (Critical for Top 5 Ranking)

### 1. Google Business Profile ⭐ (MOST IMPORTANT)
**Why:** This is THE #1 factor for local search ranking. Google Maps results dominate "car rental near me" searches.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/)
2. Click "Manage now"
3. Enter "Mok Car Rental" as your business name
4. Category: "Car Rental Service" AND "Car Rent"
5. Address: Your Johor Bahru address
6. Phone: Your WhatsApp number
7. Website: https://www.mokcarrental.com
8. Hours: Mon-Sun 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
9. Add photos: your fleet, your office, happy customers
10. Verify by postcard (takes 2-3 weeks) or phone

**After verification:**
- Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours
- Post weekly updates (new cars, promotions, travel tips)
- Add Q&A section with common questions
- Request 20+ reviews from happy customers

### 2. Submit Sitemap to Google Search Console
**Status:** Verification is complete ✅

**Steps:**
1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Select `www.mokcarrental.com` (URL prefix property)
3. Go to "Sitemaps" → Paste: `https://www.mokcarrental.com/sitemap.xml`
4. Click "Submit"
5. Go to "URL Inspection" → Paste `https://www.mokcarrental.com` → Click "Request Indexing"
6. Do the same for `/cars`, `/contact`, `/about`, `/faq`
